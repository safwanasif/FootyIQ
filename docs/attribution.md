# Data and brand attribution

FootyIQ uses StatsBomb Open Data. Source attribution and the official StatsBomb logo appear in the dashboard's model evidence section. FootyIQ is independent and does not claim affiliation or endorsement.

- Data repository: https://github.com/statsbomb/open-data
- Source terms: https://github.com/statsbomb/open-data/blob/master/LICENSE.pdf
- Pinned data revision: `4b73468fc5b0f1950f9f66fada70ad3a4f9327cb`
- Official logo source: https://github.com/statsbomb/open-data/blob/4b73468fc5b0f1950f9f66fada70ad3a4f9327cb/img/SB%20-%20Icon%20Lockup%20-%20Colour%20positive.png

The logo is stored at `apps/web/public/statsbomb-logo.png` with its original proportions and colors. It is provider-owned branding, not FootyIQ artwork. The provider's repository requests source identification and use of its logo when sharing analysis. Data and branding retain their provider terms; inclusion does not relicense them as application code.

Dataset membership, source hashes, exclusions, competition coverage and model limitations are documented in the versioned reports linked from the dashboard. The training data is selective senior men's competition coverage, not a representative sample of all football.
